2. Hosting

This website is hosted via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you use our contact form, you transmit your name, email address and your message. This data is processed via the service Formspree (Formspree, Inc., 462 1st Ave, New York, NY 10016, USA) and forwarded to us.

Formspree temporarily stores your enquiry on its servers. A data processing agreement (DPA) is in place between us and Formspree. Formspree's privacy information: formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures) and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in processing enquiries). Your data will be deleted once your enquiry has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

4. Contact by Email or Phone

If you contact us directly by email or phone, the data you transmit will be stored exclusively for the purpose of processing your enquiry and will not be passed on to third parties without your consent. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.

5. Fonts

This website uses the fonts Inter and Playfair Display. Both fonts are served locally from our server — no data is transmitted to external services such as Google. This complies with the requirements of the GDPR.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

This website uses no external tracking and no analytics tools (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel or similar). Only a single entry is set in your browser's localStorage in order to save your cookie choice (key: cookieConsent). This entry does not leave your browser and is not transmitted to third parties.
